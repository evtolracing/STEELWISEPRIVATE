# Phase 9.5: quality scorecard for the financial ledger

Scores are a judgement from the evidence named, not a measurement, on the scale of `quality-scorecard.md` (target 95). A subject with no evidence is NOT ASSESSED and gets no number. The whole-system scorecard is rebuilt by `qa/tools/excellence-docs.mjs`; the Phase 9.5 adjustments to the group "Logistics and financial operations" are in `phase-state.json`.

Everything here was verified on a local machine against a local database whose name contains `qa`, with synthetic data and a synthetic approval. No accountant has reviewed any rule, no live data was read, and the ledger has never run on the live system. Those three facts hold every score of the ledger itself below 90, however clean the test results are.
