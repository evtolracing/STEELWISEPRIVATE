# Production risk register

Written by hand in Phase 1 and updated at the end of Phase 2 and Phase 3 (all 2026-11-13). It answers one question: what stands between this code and running about 80 branches on it. Each risk points at the findings behind it (`findings-register.md`). Nothing here was measured on the live system; the live database and the host were not touched.

Likelihood and impact are a judgement from the evidence named in the row. "Blocks go-live" means the product should not carry real work for more than one branch until the risk is closed or the owner accepts it in writing.

## Overall position

**Not ready for production across branches. The database side is materially safer than at the end of Phase 2; the gaps that remain are missing functions and rules, not known defects in what exists.**

Phase 3 closed, with proof against the database: amounts stored with stray digits; 15 references on money, stock and work that nothing enforced; an order line that could be reserved twice over and a cancelled order that could keep stock for ever; lost orders when many branches save at the same moment; duplicate orders from a repeated request; a void that could stop half-way. It measured the branch screens at 85 branches and found none slow.

What still stands between this code and 80 branches:

1. **A shipment can leave with nothing reserved**, in which case no stock item or heat is recorded against it and no balance goes down (R-25, new). This is a rule for the owner.
2. **No backup has been confirmed and no restore tested** (R-11). Unchanged, and now the first thing to do.
3. **Processing moves no stock**, there is no adjustment or count, and a transfer has no in-transit stage (R-08).
4. 58 of 143 screens are labelled samples; two modules keep their own copy of stock and orders (R-03, R-04).
5. Nothing runs on a schedule; most changing routes outside order-to-cash have no success-path test.
6. Three more database updates wait for the owner (R-15).

## What is not a risk today, with the evidence

- Staff held to a branch cannot read or change another branch's orders, stock, shipments, invoices, jobs or work orders (branch boundary, 37 checks, this session).
- A branch manager cannot create, promote or change a login above their own standing (14 checks through the API, this session).
- Two operators finishing a job together, a job cancelled while it completes, a sale sent three times: the records agree afterwards (80 integrity checks, this session).
- Sign-in is required on every one of 611 routes and a customer login reaches only its own account (sweep, 20 of 20 sections, this session).
- A view-only role cannot change anything, on either address of the API (baseline probe, 540 calls, this session). This was a defect at the start of Phase 1 and is fixed.
- Overselling, double shipping, double invoicing and paying more than the total are refused, including under simultaneous requests (simulation and race probes, this session).
- Stock can never go negative and its balance always adds up: the database refuses it.
- An order line cannot be reserved beyond what it needs, and a cancelled order holds no stock, whatever the timing (6 rounds each, this session; both failed 6 of 6 before the fix).
- Eighty orders entered in the same instant are all saved, each with its own number (this session; 8 were lost before the fix).
- Money is stored to the cent, and the database refuses an invoice or order whose total is not the sum of its parts, a zero payment, and an invoice that points at a shipment that does not exist (13 refused writes, this session).
- A credit, refund or void that fails part-way leaves nothing behind (forced failure, this session).
- The branch screens answer within half a second at 85 branches, 72,240 orders and 100,233 stock items, on a small machine (this session). The host was not measured.
- No deadlock occurred in any test of the session.

## Conditions for a single-branch pilot

Unchanged in substance from Phase 1, with two additions from Phase 2 and two from Phase 3 (the rule on shipping without a reservation, R-25, is settled; the host's connection settings are confirmed, R-26). A pilot at one branch is reasonable once: all eight database files are applied (R-15); every pilot login has a home branch (R-21); a restore has been tried once (R-11); only one stock list is in use (R-03); the sample modules the pilot branch does not need are off the menu (R-04); and the web store's promise dates are either real or hidden (R-05). That is a recommendation, not a decision made here.

## Rollback

Phase 3's database changes are additive: foreign keys, checks, indexes, one new table and two new optional columns. None removes or rewrites data. The previous code runs on the new schema, so the pull request can be reverted without touching the database. Each constraint can be dropped by name with no effect on data. Detail: `phase-03-migration-review.md`, section 5.

Phase 2 (kept for the record): one column, one index and one foreign key (`Order.locationId`), all additive. Pull request 30 was merged on the owner's instruction on 2026-11-13 and is live.
