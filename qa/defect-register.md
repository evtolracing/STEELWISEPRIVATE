# Defect register

Severity: **P0** stops the core business or corrupts records. **P1** a main workflow is wrong, missing or unsafe. **P2** a secondary feature is broken or a control is weak. **P3** minor.

Status: **Fixed** means repaired in this pull request or the one before it, with a regression test or a simulation check that fails without the fix. **Open** means not repaired, with the reason.

Evidence files are in `qa/results/`. "Sim" is `qa/sim/run.mjs`, "Sweep" is `qa/sim/security.mjs`, "Unit" is `src/backend/test`.

## Rules this change introduces

These are now enforced by the server. Each was chosen to match what the screens already assume, but they are business rules and should be confirmed.

1. An order becomes PARTIAL or COMPLETE only by shipping. Cancelled and completed orders cannot be changed.
2. A partly shipped order cannot be cancelled or put on hold.
3. Only CONFIRMED or PARTIAL orders can have shipments.
4. A shipment's lines cannot exceed what is left on the order, counting other shipments still on the dock.
5. A tracked stock item transfers whole. Moving part of one is a processing job.
6. Stock can only be received at the location where the item already is.
7. Shipping takes stock from what was reserved for the order line. A line with nothing reserved ships without changing stock.
8. Job moves follow the job board's own table. A job that has started cannot be moved to another order.
9. A new job starts as ORDERED, SCHEDULED or ON_HOLD.
10. An order needs a customer or at least one line.
11. Dispatching a shipment invoices what shipped. Terms come from the order, then the customer; with no terms the invoice is due in 30 days. Freight is billed on the first invoice. Tax follows the order's tax rate.
12. A payment cannot be more than the invoice balance. A paid or cancelled invoice takes no payment. Only an invoice with no payments can be cancelled.
13. A credit is applied to the open balance first. Anything beyond that is owed back to the customer until a refund is recorded.
14. A return can only be opened against shipped quantity, and not for more than shipped less already returned. Each line is restocked or scrapped. The credit is issued when the material is received, not when the return is opened.
15. Quick sale is for items priced each. Material priced by weight is rung up as a counter order. A walk-in with no account is filed under "Walk-in / Cash Sale".
16. Credits, refunds, cancelling an invoice and voiding a quick sale need a manager (administrator, leadership, credit manager or sales director).
17. Staff can change only their department's records (table in D-018). Everyone can still read.
18. A quote is valid 30 days unless a date is given. A draft quote can be accepted directly. Accepting one closes the other open quotes on the same RFQ.
19. Web checkout always saves the system price for the customer's price level.
20. A division login sees its own division only. A head-office login sees all divisions.
21. A line with an unfinished shop job cannot be dispatched.
