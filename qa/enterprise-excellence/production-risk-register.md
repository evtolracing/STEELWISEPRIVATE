# Production risk register

Written by hand in Phase 1 and updated at the end of Phase 2 (both 2026-11-13). It answers one question: what stands between this code and running about 80 branches on it. Each risk points at the findings behind it (`findings-register.md`). Nothing here was measured on the live system; the live database and the host were not touched.

Likelihood and impact are a judgement from the evidence named in the row. "Blocks go-live" means the product should not carry real work for more than one branch until the risk is closed or the owner accepts it in writing.

## Overall position

**Not ready for production across branches, and closer than at the end of Phase 1.** Phase 2 closed the two risks that made more than one branch unsafe: staff are now held to their own branches on the server (R-01) and every changing route has an owning department (R-02). It also closed the way a branch manager could become super admin, and the ways the shop-floor record went wrong when two people acted at once.

What still stands between this code and 80 branches: 58 of 143 screens are labelled samples (the owner has decided to build them all); two modules keep their own copy of stock and orders (decided, not yet connected or retired); nothing runs on a schedule; most changing routes outside order-to-cash and the shop floor have no test that makes them succeed; and five database updates wait for the owner.

## What is not a risk today, with the evidence

- Staff held to a branch cannot read or change another branch's orders, stock, shipments, invoices, jobs or work orders (branch boundary, 37 checks, this session).
- A branch manager cannot create, promote or change a login above their own standing (14 checks through the API, this session).
- Two operators finishing a job together, a job cancelled while it completes, a sale sent three times: the records agree afterwards (80 integrity checks, this session).
- Sign-in is required on every one of 611 routes and a customer login reaches only its own account (sweep, 20 of 20 sections, this session).
- A view-only role cannot change anything, on either address of the API (baseline probe, 540 calls, this session). This was a defect at the start of Phase 1 and is fixed.
- Overselling, double shipping, double invoicing and paying more than the total are refused, including under simultaneous requests (simulation and race probes, this session).
- Stock can never go negative and its balance always adds up: the database refuses it.

## Conditions for a single-branch pilot

Unchanged in substance from Phase 1, with two additions from Phase 2. A pilot at one branch is reasonable once: all five database files are applied (R-15); every pilot login has a home branch (R-21); a restore has been tried once (R-11); only one stock list is in use (R-03); the sample modules the pilot branch does not need are off the menu (R-04); and the web store's promise dates are either real or hidden (R-05). That is a recommendation, not a decision made here.

## Rollback

Phase 2 added one column, one index and one foreign key (`Order.locationId`), all additive, and changed no existing data except to fill that column. Reverting the Phase 2 commits restores the earlier behaviour (no branch boundary, the open login routes, the shop-floor races); the column can stay. Pull request 30 is not merged, so nothing in it is live.
