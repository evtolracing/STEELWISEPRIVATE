# Phase 10: quality scorecard for security, financial integrity, reliability and recovery

Scores are a judgement from the evidence named, not a measurement, on the scale of `quality-scorecard.md` (target 95). A subject with no evidence is NOT ASSESSED and gets no number. The whole-system scorecard is rebuilt by `qa/tools/excellence-docs.mjs`; the Phase 10 adjustments are in `phase-state.json`.

Everything here was verified on a local machine against local databases whose names contain `qa`, with synthetic data, one server process and a stub in place of every outside service. Nothing was run against the live database, the host or any real partner. Three facts hold the scores of the live-facing subjects down however clean the tests are: database update 17 is not applied on the live system, the live application still connects as the database owner, and the host's own backups have never been restored.
